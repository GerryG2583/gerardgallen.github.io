# About Me

Senior Technical Writer and Editor with more than 10 years of experience creating user guides, API documentation, online help, and developer content for enterprise software.

I bring strong editorial judgement, information design skills, and technical depth. My focus is translating complexity into clarity—whether I am authoring new documentation, improving legacy content, or designing scalable publishing and governance processes for complex digital products.

## Background

I have a BA in Journalism and English from Staffordshire University and a Diploma in Audio and Moving Image from the North West Institute. My career spans editorial work, journalism, and technical writing across multiple industries, including aviation IT, analytics, telecommunications, and enterprise software.

I combine editorial excellence with technical depth to produce documentation that is clear, usable, and aligned to real user needs. I am particularly interested in roles where strong documentation quality, governance, and information design contribute directly to user experience and product success.
