# About Me

Senior Technical Writer and Editor with 10+ years of experience creating online help, user guides, API documentation, and developer content for enterprise software products.

I specialise in content strategy, information architecture, and documentation modernisation. I combine editorial excellence with technical depth to deliver clear, usable, and maintainable documentation in complex, fast-moving environments.

## Professional Summary

My career spans editorial work, journalism, and technical writing across multiple industries and organisations. I've led documentation initiatives, managed complex content transformations, and improved documentation workflows through process innovation and emerging technologies.

I'm skilled in structured authoring, Agile methodologies, content strategy, and documentation lifecycle management. I've worked with MadCap Flare, DITA CMS, Oxygen Editor, and Markdown-based workflows. I'm also experienced in building AI-assisted documentation processes and collaborating with cross-functional teams to deliver high-quality content.
