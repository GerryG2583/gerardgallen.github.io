# About Me

Senior Technical Writer and Editor with more than 10 years of experience creating user guides, API documentation, online help, and developer content for enterprise software.

I bring strong editorial judgement, information design skills, and technical depth. My focus is translating complexity into clarity—whether I am authoring new documentation, improving legacy content, or shaping the documentation strategy behind a product.

[Download CV](Gerard%20Gallen%20CV%202026.docx)

## Core Strengths

- Information architecture and content strategy
- Structured authoring (DITA, Markdown, XML)
- Documentation governance and lifecycle management
- Agile documentation delivery and sprint-based collaboration
- Editorial leadership and quality improvement
- Cross-functional communication and stakeholder alignment
- AI-assisted documentation workflows and process automation

## Tools & Technologies

MadCap Flare, Oxygen XML Editor, DITA CMS, Markdown, Confluence, Jira, Azure DevOps, Git, GitHub, Acrolinx, Adobe Acrobat, Microsoft Office, SharePoint

## Background

I have a BA in Journalism and English from Staffordshire University and a Diploma in Audio and Moving Image from the North West Institute. My career spans editorial work, journalism, and technical communication, giving me a strong mix of storytelling, clarity, and structured process.

I combine editorial excellence with technical depth to produce documentation that is clear, usable, and aligned to real user needs. I am particularly interested in roles where strong documentation supports product adoption, customer success, and operational efficiency.
